title: "Nonlinear Einstein evolution with hyperboloidal scri-fixing for gravitational-wave astronomy"
abstract: >
  Accurate predictions of gravitational waves from merging black holes are essential for interpreting observations and testing general relativity.
  Numerical simulations typically cover a finite region, while gravitational radiation is most naturally described at null infinity.
  I will present a new implementation of the nonlinear Einstein equations that brings null infinity onto a finite computational grid.
  Building on Nützi’s regular formulation, the method uses hyperboloidal scri-fixing to keep this radiation boundary at a fixed grid location.
  Demonstrations include wave extraction from a binary-black-hole merger, nonlinear gravitational-wave memory, and coupled interior and exterior evolution of perturbed black holes.
  I will discuss how these developments can reduce boundary and wave-extraction errors in numerical relativity and support more accurate astrophysical waveform modeling.
